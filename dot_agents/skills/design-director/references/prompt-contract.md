# Design prompt contract

Complete this for directed work. Short answers are better than vague prose.

```md
## Design contract

Status: draft | locked | accepted
Design lead: <model/session>
Mode: greenfield | unfamiliar audit | preserve | targeted | overhaul

### Outcome
- User and job:
- Desired user-visible result:
- Success evidence:

### Context
- Product/platform:
- Existing system to preserve:
- Repo constraints:
- Content/data available:

### Direction
- One-sentence concept:
- Hierarchy and primary action:
- Density, rhythm, and composition:
- Type, color, shape, and imagery:
- Motion character and purpose:
- References — take:
- References — reject:
- Explicit anti-goals:

### Behavior
- Main flow:
- Required states:
- Responsive/resizing behavior:
- Keyboard, focus, screen-reader, contrast, and reduced-motion needs:

### Implementation boundary
- Reuse:
- May change:
- Must not change:
- Design deviations require:

### Acceptance proof
- Target sizes/platforms:
- Screens/states to capture:
- Interaction or motion to inspect:
- Automated checks:
```

Locking means the design lead has resolved the meaningful aesthetic choices. Acceptance means the rendered result was inspected against this contract.
